User guide

Understand the platform in 4 simple rules

This page is written for users. It explains how to post, learn, discuss, report, and stay safe on X Horizon.

01

Be respectful

Community

Ask questions, debate ideas, and correct mistakes without insulting, humiliating, threatening, or targeting anyone.

02

Keep it space-related

Relevance

Posts should connect to astronomy, space science, missions, telescopes, observations, simulations, courses, tools, or X Horizon features.

03

Do not mislead people

Accuracy

Big claims need sources. Fiction, theories, AI images, and guesses must be labeled clearly so users do not confuse them with real science.

04

Protect safety and privacy

Safety

Never share private information or unsafe instructions involving lasers, Sun viewing, rockets, batteries, chemicals, or dangerous equipment.

Before posting

Check your post before sharing

Use this checklist to make sure your post helps other users instead of confusing them.

Is your post related to space, science, observation, missions, or X Horizon?
Did you add enough context so others can understand your question or answer?
If you make a big claim, did you include a reliable source or say it is only your opinion?
If your content includes an image, is it real, relevant, and not misleading?
Did you avoid sharing personal information, private messages, passwords, exact addresses, or student details?
Does your post avoid dangerous instructions or unsafe equipment advice?
Reports

How to report a problem

Reporting helps keep the platform safe. Report only real rule problems, not people you simply disagree with.

01

Choose the reason

Pick the closest reason: harassment, unsafe advice, fake science, scam link, private information, copied content, or off-topic spam.

Report
02

Add short context

Explain what rule is broken. Keep it simple: what happened, where it happened, and why it is risky.

Explain
03

The report is reviewed

X Horizon checks the post, context, source links, severity, and whether the user has repeated the same issue.

Review
04

Action is taken if needed

The post may be corrected, hidden, removed, or the account may be restricted if the issue is serious.
