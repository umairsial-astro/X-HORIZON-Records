About Me

I'm Umair Sial, a BS Physics student at the Pakistan Institute of Engineering and Applied Sciences (PIEAS), with a strong interest in astronomy, astrophysics, space science, mathematics, programming, and scientific communication.

Alongside my academic journey, I am building X HORIZON — an independent astronomy ecosystem designed to make astronomy more accessible through learning, practical exploration, scientific resources, projects, visuals, and community.

Beyond physics, I'm drawn to computer science, AI, web development and aerospace technology. I build for the web with HTML, CSS and Odoo customisation, and I write the educational content myself.

My long-term goal is to develop strong foundations in physics and mathematics and eventually contribute to advanced astronomy, astrophysics, or space-science research.

Explore Nature Beyond the Horizon.

X HORIZON is an independent astronomy platform founded by Umair Sial to create a free, accessible ecosystem for people interested in astronomy and space science.

The platform brings together astronomy learning, scientific concepts, practical skills, visual resources, career guidance, projects, community interaction, simulations, space resources and opportunities — including live space events, learning systems and space-tech awareness.
