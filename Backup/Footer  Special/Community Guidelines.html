Report flow

How reports should move

Reports should be handled in a predictable order, not randomly.

01

User reports content

The report reason should say what happened: harassment, unsafe instruction, fake claim, spam, copied content, or privacy issue.

02

Moderator checks context

Review the content, surrounding replies, source links, severity, and whether it is a first-time mistake or repeated abuse.

03

Action matches severity

Use edit request, warning, hiding, temporary restriction, or removal. Critical safety/privacy abuse needs immediate action.

04

Record the reason

Keep a clear note so appeals, repeated issues, and future moderation decisions stay fair.

Before posting

Community quality checklist

Use this list before posting a claim, guide, image, simulation, or correction.

Is the post clearly related to space, astronomy, missions, observation, or X Horizon learning?
Does the claim need a source, calculation, date, equipment detail, or uncertainty note?
Could the content create safety risk with the Sun, lasers, rockets, batteries, or pressure systems?
Is the title clear enough for search and navigation?
Are images, datasets, mission photos, or copied text credited correctly?
Does the post help learners instead of just adding noise?
