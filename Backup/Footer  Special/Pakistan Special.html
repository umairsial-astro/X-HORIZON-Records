Climate maps

Built from monthly mean temperature and rainfall at 23 stations, interpolated by inverse-distance weighting. Temperature is first reduced to sea level, interpolated, then lowered again by 6.5 °C per kilometre of modelled terrain height. Treat values between stations as estimates, especially in the far north.

Climate zones

Classified per pixel with the Köppen scheme from the modelled monthly fields, then merged into nine classes suited to Pakistan. The outline is schematic and shows Pakistan-administered territory.

Live feeds

Weather comes from Open-Meteo, the ISS position from wheretheiss.at and launches from Launch Library 2. When a feed cannot be reached the page says so and falls back to the calculated model. Moon phases use Meeus' algorithm, good to a few minutes.
