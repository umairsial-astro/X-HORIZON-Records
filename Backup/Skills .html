The skills behind every stargazer

They aren't ranked, and you don't need all nine to start. Together, though, they cover most of what separates admiring the sky from actually studying it.

Space Data Analysis

Open Python for Space Science

Satellite Operations

Explore satellite operations, spacecraft status, orbital passes, telemetry and the procedures used to operate space systems.

Open Satellite Operations

Orbital Mechanics

Scientific Writing

Build clear scientific explanations and reports, structure evidence, and communicate research so others can understand and evaluate it.

Mathematics

Strengthen the mathematical tools used across space science, including functions, calculus, equations and quantitative problem-solving.
