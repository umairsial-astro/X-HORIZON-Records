Space field routes

Use this section to decide whether you need an engineering degree, physics degree, data route, electronics route or Earth-observation route.

Aerospace & Astronautics

Best for spacecraft, launch vehicles, aerodynamics, propulsion, orbital mechanics, structures, guidance and mission engineering.

  • Core: calculus, mechanics, thermodynamics, programming
  • Projects: rockets, drones, simulations, CAD/CFD
  • Career direction: spacecraft engineer, GNC, propulsion, systems
Astrophysics & Astronomy

Best for stars, galaxies, black holes, cosmology, telescopes, observations, simulations and scientific research.

  • Core: physics, calculus, statistics, Python
  • Projects: telescope data, simulations, image processing
  • Career direction: researcher, data scientist, observatory scientist
Planetary Science

Best for Mars, Moon, asteroids, exoplanets, planetary atmospheres, geology, astrobiology and mission science.

  • Core: physics, geology, chemistry, data
  • Projects: crater mapping, spectra, Mars/Moon datasets
  • Career direction: planetary researcher, mission scientist
Satellites & Space Systems

Best for CubeSats, payloads, communications, electronics, operations, mission control and spacecraft subsystems.

  • Core: electronics, coding, control systems, systems engineering
  • Projects: CubeSat, ground station, telemetry dashboard
  • Career direction: satellite engineer, mission analyst
Remote Sensing

Best for Earth observation, climate, agriculture, disasters, GIS, radar, optical satellites and data products.

  • Core: geospatial data, Python, statistics, physics
  • Projects: Sentinel/Landsat analysis, GIS dashboards
  • Career direction: EO analyst, geospatial scientist
Avionics & Robotics

Best for embedded systems, flight computers, sensors, robotics, autonomy and control software.

  • Core: electronics, C/C++, Python, controls
  • Projects: rover, drone, sensor package, autopilot
  • Career direction: avionics engineer, robotics engineer
Space Policy & Business

Best for students who want law, economics, policy, strategy, space sustainability and space-sector management.

  • Core: policy, economics, communication, systems thinking
  • Projects: space law notes, debris policy, mission business case
  • Career direction: policy analyst, space program manager
For countries without space degrees

Start with physics, mechanical engineering, electronics, computer science, GIS, meteorology or mathematics, then specialize through a master’s.

  • Core: local degree + online portfolio
  • Projects: satellite data, simulations, Arduino sensors
  • Career direction: regional master’s, internship, research lab
