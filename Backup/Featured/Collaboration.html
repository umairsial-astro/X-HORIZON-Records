Who Can Collaborate

X Horizon collaboration is for platforms and people who can strengthen the public understanding of space science, exploration, simulations, astronomy tools, and future careers.

☄

Space Content Platforms

Pages, websites, and channels that publish astronomy, space missions, planetary science, telescope learning, or science explainers.

✦

Educational Communities

Student groups, learning societies, school clubs, university circles, and science communities that guide learners in a decent environment.

◌

Simulation & Tools Builders

Creators building sky maps, orbital systems, celestial simulations, data dashboards, learning tools, or interactive science pages.

▣

Research Learners

Individuals or groups sharing research notes, mission studies, observation projects, astrophysics concepts, or scientific resources.

⌁

Media Creators

Video creators, article writers, visual designers, and educational storytellers focused on authentic space knowledge and future exploration.

◎

Opportunity Networks

Platforms sharing scholarships, internships, events, competitions, university paths, or career guidance related to astronomy and space science.

Review Path

Each application enters a controlled review flow. The daily gate keeps submissions organized, numbered, and limited to 50 applications within each midnight-to-midnight daily cycle.

Submit Platform

The collaborator provides platform name, link, category, purpose, and proof that the content supports space learning.

Check Eligibility

X Horizon checks content type, safety, behavior, authenticity, and whether the platform matches the collaboration rules.

Review Quality

The platform is reviewed for scientific value, useful presentation, respectful community conduct, and clean public image.

Approve Connection

Approved collaborators can be listed, linked, or connected with X Horizon areas according to the collaboration purpose.

Rules Before Applying

Click the rules button to reveal the full collaboration code of conduct. These rules protect X Horizon, users, creators, and public trust.

Professional Space Network

Collaboration is not for random promotion. It is for serious platforms that add value to astronomy, space education, science learning, research awareness, simulations, or future space careers.

Important Standard

X Horizon may reject, pause, or remove any collaboration if the linked platform breaks safety, behavior, content, privacy, or authenticity rules. A clean platform is more important than a large audience.

01

Space Content Required

The platform available to link must promote space, astronomy, physics, simulations, missions, research, science tools, or educational learning.

02

Decent Behavior

Collaborators must maintain respectful language, professional interaction, and decent behavior on X Horizon and connected community spaces.

03

Authentic Knowledge

No fake discoveries, misleading claims, copied posts without credit, false mission updates, or unverified scientific statements.

04

Safe Public Links

Linked pages must not contain malware, scams, gambling, adult content, hate content, spam farms, or harmful redirects.

05

Privacy Protection

No collaborator can misuse, expose, sell, collect, or pressure users for private information through X Horizon collaboration.

06

No Harassment

Bullying, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, or abusive community behavior are not accepted.

07

Credit Sources

Images, mission facts, research data, telescope material, and external resources should be credited when required.

08

No False Representation

A collaborator must not claim official X Horizon authority unless that role is clearly approved by X Horizon.

09

Quality Standard

The platform should have a clear purpose, readable design, useful content, and a serious educational direction.

10

Daily Limit Respect

Only 50 collaboration forms are accepted per day. Attempts to bypass the limit may cause rejection.

11

No Forced Promotion

Collaboration must not pressure X Horizon users into joining external groups, buying services, or following unrelated pages.

12

X Horizon Review Rights

X Horizon can approve, reject, remove, or recheck any collaboration to protect the platform’s mission and users.
