Islam & Space Science

A collection of Qur'anic ayat that touch on the heavens, the stars, and the order of the cosmos — each shown with its exact reference, the original Arabic text, an English translation, and a short reflection linking it to a space-science theme.

Curated by Umair Sial  ·  Last updated: August 31, 2026

A note on approach — each entry below pairs the ayah with a widely used English translation (Sahih International) and a short reflection drawn from tafsir (Qur'anic commentary) and later commentary on the natural world. These reflections are offered as devotional and educational reading, not as scientific proofs, and readers are encouraged to consult qualified scholars for deeper study.
